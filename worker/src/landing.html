How it works

Four steps. One of them is typing a slash.

No sign-up, no OAuth and no uploading your chats. One global pool of builders, wherever you are. Works in Claude Code, Codex and claude.ai. Your own agent does the profiling, on your own machine, with its own reasoning.

01

Install

One command installs a single binary into ~/.slopmatch and adds the /slopmatch skill to your agent.

02

Get read

Your agent goes through your recent transcripts and skills: planner or diver, big delegator or tight steerer, 2am shipper or 9-to-5.

03

Approve & publish

You see the exact payload first: a pseudonymous card and ten numbers. Nothing goes up until you say yes.

04

Match & reveal

See why you'd click and where you'd clash. Contacts swap only if you both reveal.

Privacy

Your slop stays home.

The server never sees a single prompt. It gets ten numbers and a card you've read and approved.

🔒
Profiled locallyTranscripts, file paths, project names and skill contents never leave your machine.

Dry run firstPublish prints the exact JSON before anything is sent. Your agent has to show it to you.
🎭
Double-blind revealYou're a pseudonym until two people pick each other. One-sided interest stays invisible.

Forget meslopmatch delete --yes wipes your profile and reveals from the server.
